- **Anime4K shaders**: [bloc97](https://github.com/bloc97/Anime4K) and contributors (MIT). The restore, upscale, denoise and line-effect networks are theirs, unchanged.
- **mpv** — the `//!HOOK` / `//!BIND` user-shader format the shaders are written in, which this extension reads.
