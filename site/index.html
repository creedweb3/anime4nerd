ep 02 · how it works

Five passes, every frame.

bloc97's Anime4K shaders, copied unchanged from the open-source project. Anime4Nerd reads their mpv shader format and runs each pass in WebGL2, once per decoded video frame.

  1. pass 01

    Read the frame

    The current frame is copied from the page's video into GPU memory.

    upload · 0.3 ms
  2. pass 02

    Clamp highlights

    Limits bright overshoot so later passes don't ring around edges.

    clamp · 0.6 ms
  3. pass 03

    Restore

    A small neural network repairs blur and compression on lines.

    cnn · 6.1 ms
  4. pass 04

    Upscale ×2

    A second network doubles width and height, predicting the new pixels.

    cnn ×2 · 7.4 ms
  5. pass 05

    Overlay

    The result is drawn on a canvas laid exactly over the player.

    draw · 0.7 ms

ep 04 · fullscreen & upscale

Bigger, in fullscreen.

The toolbar popup can't open over a fullscreen video, so a small menu opens inside the player instead. It also sets how far to upscale.

the ☰ menu

Change it without leaving fullscreen

Move the mouse and a ☰ button appears in the top-right corner of the player. It opens the on/off switch, presets, line effects, the upscale factor and a live GPU graph, and fades away when the mouse rests. Clicks on the menu never reach the player, so the video keeps playing.

option b · manual

From the package

  1. Download anime4nerd.zip (sha256) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Play a video, open the extension, and switch the video on.

ep 06 · questions

Questions.

Short answers to what people ask most.

Does it work on live action or regular shows?

It runs on any video, but it is made for anime. The Anime4K networks are trained on hand-drawn animation: clean line art and flat colour. On live action, films and 3D-animated shows the result can look over-sharpened or painted, so the browser's normal scaling usually looks better there.

Does it work on every site?

It works on any site where the video is served with cross-origin access, which most players do. DRM-protected video (Widevine) cannot be read by any extension, so it plays normally.

What do I need?

Chrome, Brave or Edge 116 or newer and a GPU with WebGL2. A mid-range GPU handles Mode A at 24 fps comfortably.

Can it upscale more than 2×?

Yes. The Anime4K networks are trained for exactly 2×, so 4× and up chain extra 2× passes. Auto picks 4× when a small video fills a big screen, like 720p fullscreen on a 1440p or 4K display. You can also choose up to 32×; Anime4Nerd uses the largest factor your GPU can hold (8K output at most) and says when it capped. Above 4× the browser shrinks most of the extra detail back to your screen size.

Can I limit it to some sites?

Yes. In chrome://extensions, open Anime4Nerd's details and set Site access to "On specific sites".

ep 07 · version history

What's new.

After an update the toolbar icon shows a NEW badge, and the popup links here.

1.1.0

latestOctober 2026
  • Upscale beyond 2×. Pick auto, 2×, 4×, 8×, 16× or 32×. Auto uses 4× when a small video fills a big screen, like 720p in fullscreen on a 1440p or 4K display. Higher choices run at the largest factor your GPU can hold, and the popup says when it capped.
  • A menu inside fullscreen. Move the mouse and a ☰ button appears in the corner of the player: switch it on or off, change preset, line effects and upscale factor, and watch the GPU graph without leaving fullscreen.
  • Exact output size on each video card, like 720p → 5120×2880 (4×).
  • Clean restarts after an update. The old copy of the extension no longer keeps drawing on open pages or throws "Extension context invalidated" errors.
  • Security pass on the new parts, including tests that play the role of a hostile page.

1.0.1

October 2026
  • Works on DVD-era anime. Older shows mastered from DVD often use non-square pixels, and on those the upscaled picture stayed frozen or blank. They now upscale like everything else.
  • Rotated and odd-sized videos are handled correctly.
  • Clear errors instead of a stuck picture. If the GPU rejects a frame, Anime4Nerd now switches back to the normal video and tells you why.
  • Better overlay placement on players that crop the video, pin it to a corner, sit in a scrolling box, or move the video between layouts.
  • Picture-in-picture and fullscreen now pause the upscaler on players built with shadow DOM too.
  • Lighter on busy pages. At most two videos per page are upscaled at once; playing and larger videos go first, the rest wait.
  • Update notice. After an update, a small NEW badge and a one-line note in the popup link here, so you can see what changed.
  • Fixed quick setting toggles overwriting each other, and popup switches that could stop updating after a drag.

October 2026
  • First release: real-time 2× Anime4K upscaling for any web video, six presets, optional line darkening and thinning, per-site on/off.
ep 08 · privacy

Privacy.

Anime4Nerd does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Video frames are processed on your GPU and discarded. Your settings stay in your browser's extension storage. The extension makes no network requests of its own.
