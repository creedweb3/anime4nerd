ep 02 · how it works

Five passes, every frame.

bloc97's Anime4K shaders, copied unchanged from the open-source project. Anime4Nerd reads their mpv shader format and runs each pass in WebGL2, once per decoded video frame.

How it works, in depth→shaders · colour · manga · limits

  1. pass 01

    Read the frame

    The current frame is copied from the page's video into GPU memory.

    upload · 0.3 ms
  2. pass 02

    Clamp highlights

    Limits bright overshoot so later passes don't ring around edges.

    clamp · 0.6 ms
  3. pass 03

    Restore

    A small neural network repairs blur and compression on lines.

    cnn · 6.1 ms
  4. pass 04

    Upscale ×2

    A second network doubles width and height, predicting the new pixels.

    cnn ×2 · 7.4 ms
  5. pass 05

    Overlay

    The result is drawn on a canvas laid exactly over the player.

    draw · 0.7 ms

ep 04 · fullscreen & upscale

Bigger, in fullscreen.

The toolbar popup can't open over a fullscreen video, so a small menu opens inside the player instead. It also sets how far to upscale.

the ☰ menu

Change it without leaving fullscreen

Move the mouse and a ☰ button appears in the top-right corner of the player. It opens the on/off switch, presets, line effects, the upscale factor and a live GPU graph, and fades away when the mouse rests. Clicks on the menu never reach the player, so the video keeps playing.

ep 08 · questions

Questions.

Short answers to what people ask most.

How does it work?

It's not an AI tool. Anime4Nerd uses shaders on your GPU to clean up each frame of the video as it plays: the blur goes away and the lines get sharper. It doesn't generate or redraw anything; it improves the picture you're already watching, on any site, and the video never leaves your computer.

Does it work on live action or regular shows?

It runs on any video, but it is made for anime. The Anime4K networks are trained on hand-drawn animation: clean line art and flat colour. On live action, films and 3D-animated shows the result can look over-sharpened or painted, so the browser's normal scaling usually looks better there.

Does it work on every site?

It works on any site where the video is served with cross-origin access, which most players do. DRM-protected video (Widevine) cannot be read by any extension, so it plays normally.

What do I need?

Chrome, Brave or Edge 116 or newer (or Firefox 140 or newer) and a GPU with WebGL2. A mid-range GPU handles Mode A at 24 fps comfortably.

Can it upscale more than 2×?

Yes. The Anime4K networks are trained for exactly 2×, so 4× and up chain extra 2× passes. Auto picks 4× when a small video fills a big screen, like 720p fullscreen on a 1440p or 4K display. You can also choose up to 8×; Anime4Nerd uses the largest factor your GPU can hold (8K output at most), steps down while your GPU can't keep up, and says when it capped. Above 4× the browser shrinks most of the extra detail back to your screen size.

Can I limit it to some sites?

Yes. In chrome://extensions, open Anime4Nerd's details and set Site access to "On specific sites". In Firefox, use about:addons → Anime4Nerd → Permissions.

ep 10 · privacy

Privacy.

Anime4Nerd does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Video frames are processed on your GPU and discarded. Your settings stay in your browser's extension storage. The extension sends nothing about you; its only network requests are in manga mode, downloading the page images your manga reader already shows. The bug icon in the popup and the page shown after removal open optional forms on this site, which send only what you write, when you press send.
