anime4k · live · on your gpu

Sharper anime,
right in your browser.

Real-time 2×–8× upscaling for any web video. Flip one switch and the original Anime4K networks restore and upscale every frame as it plays.

  • 1080p → 2160p and beyond, cleaner lines
  • Videos, embedded players, and manga readers
  • Six presets, line effects, color controls
  • Video never leaves your machine
Runs bloc97's open-source Anime4K shaders · WebGL2 · Chrome, Brave, Edge

new in 1.1

Change it without
leaving fullscreen.

Move the mouse and a ☰ button appears in the player's corner. Switch it on or off, pick a preset or line effect, and set how far to upscale.

  • Upscale: auto, 2×, 4×, 8× and up
  • Exact output size, and a note if your GPU caps it
  • Live GPU graph while you choose
  • Works in embedded players and F11 too

new in 1.3 · manga mode

Manga pages,
upscaled too.

Open a chapter on your manga reader and switch it on. Every page is restored and upscaled on your GPU, once per chapter.

  • Page by page, while you read
  • Page map: see what's done, jump to any page
  • Alt+M shows the originals
  • Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox
Mode setting: auto, anime or manga · the popup suggests a switch when a page holds the other kind
Anime4Nerd
new in 1.3 · color

Tune the
colors.

Ten controls in VLC's units, five ready-made looks and your own saved profiles, in the popup and the fullscreen menu.

  • Brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma, hue
  • Vibrance, temperature, tint, deband, grain
  • Save a look, switch with one tap
  • Also works on videos you don't upscale
Applied in the final pass on your GPU, with an 8-bit dither
Anime4Nerd

Private by design.

Everything happens on your own GPU. No account, no tracking, and nothing about you is sent anywhere.

network

Nothing about you

No analytics, telemetry or remote code. Video frames never leave your machine. Manga mode only downloads the page images your reader already shows.

control

You switch it on

Nothing is upscaled until you turn a video or reader on. Restrict it to chosen sites any time in browser settings.

open source

Original Anime4K

bloc97's MIT-licensed shaders, unchanged, run locally through WebGL2.
