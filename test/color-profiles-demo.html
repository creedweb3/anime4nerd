the profiles: from shared presets

No one publishes color presets for watching anime video; the only shared, concrete "anime look" grades are ReShade presets for anime-style games. Each profile below is one of those grades, converted to our controls from the shaders' source code, plus deband and grain from the Kokomins mpv anime guide. The presets' LUTs, bloom and tonemapping cannot be converted, so these carry their main grade, not every detail. Every profile changes at least half of the ten controls.
