color controls in the fullscreen menu (design options)

Each screen runs the real fullscreen menu (src/fullscreen-menu.ts) over a picture drawn by the real renderer (Anime4K and the color shader). Every control works and changes the picture. Open the menu with ☰ in the top-right corner. The mouse going idle fades the menu, as it does in the extension.
